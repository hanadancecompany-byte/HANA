HANA was born from a passion for dance, creativity and the power of artistic expression.

What begins with movement and choreography is envisioned to grow into something much greater — a creative arts company bringing together dance, music, theatre, culture and diverse artistic disciplines.

02 / FOUNDER & CREATIVE DIRECTOR

The vision
behind HANA.

Venu Krishaanth

With over nine years of experience in choreography and performance direction, Venu Krishaanth has built his creative journey around movement, music and the art of storytelling.

From choreographing individual performances to directing large-scale productions, his work has reached audiences through hundreds of performances and collaborations with schools across Bengaluru.

Having directed productions involving more than 250 performers, he brings together artistic vision, creative planning and on-stage execution.

HANA represents the next chapter of that journey — a vision to create a home for creative expression, bring artistic disciplines together and build opportunities for artists to explore, create and grow.

“Every expression has a story. My vision is to create spaces where those stories can come alive.”

03 / OUR PHILOSOPHY

A home for
creative expression.

We believe creativity has no single language, and art has no boundaries.

It lives in movement, music, theatre, design, culture and the many ways people express what they feel and imagine.

We believe every individual has a creative voice worth discovering, every idea deserves the opportunity to grow, and every form of art has the power to connect people.

HANA exists to bring these expressions together — creating a space where creativity is encouraged, talent is nurtured and imagination finds its place in the world.

OUR BELIEF Expression has no boundaries.
